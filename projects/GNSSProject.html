GPS-Signal Decoding with GNSS-SDR and RTL-SDR

Chinmay Deo, Dr. Sukwinder Singh · Signal Corps, NIT Jalandhar · 2024

← Back to project archive

Project summary

Abstract

My job in this project was to validate our in-house MATLAB GPS-signal receiver's acquisition module and cross-check CAF plots for detected satellites. I learnt to operate RTL-SDRs and use live raw GNSS data to decode my own position with a GPS antenna to understand the process. The demo video shows the first time I had acquired a signal lock, sitting in front of my department on a chilly november evening. I also learnt to build custom configuration files for the Linux equivalent of GNSS-SDR to post-process signals recorded via a simple flowgraph in GNU Radio.
